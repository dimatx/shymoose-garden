'Mushroom' is a dwarf form of Japanese cedar selected for its unusually broad,
rounded silhouette — more a layered dome than the tall spire most people
associate with *Cryptomeria japonica*. The fine awl-like needles give it a
soft, almost heather-like texture that reads beautifully in rock gardens,
troughs, and small conifer collections.

Through spring and summer the foliage is fresh green, then cool weather brings
on the reddish-bronze tones that make this cultivar especially appealing in
winter. It grows slowly and steadily, so the plant keeps its neat shape for a
long time without constant clipping.

Site it in sun or light shade with rich, well-drained soil and protection from
dry winter wind. Like many dwarf conifers, it resents both waterlogged roots
and repeated hard pruning. Given a sheltered spot and steady moisture, it is an
easy specimen for small spaces where year-round structure matters.
