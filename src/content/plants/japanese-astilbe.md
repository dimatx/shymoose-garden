*Astilbe japonica* 'Rheinland' tops neat, deep green foliage with clear pink
flower plumes in late spring — a shade perennial with over a century of
garden history behind it and still going strong.

## An astilbe with a long history

Introduced in 1902 by the German nurseryman Georg Arends, 'Rheinland' was
among the very first *Astilbe japonica* hybrids ever bred. Its clean pink
color and reliable performance kept it in commerce ever since, long
outlasting most of its contemporaries.

## Care tips

Site in part to full shade with rich, consistently moist soil — like all
astilbes, it declines quickly if allowed to dry out, showing crisp, brown
leaf edges. Mulch helps hold moisture through summer heat. Leave the spent
plumes standing for winter texture or cut them back for tidiness, and divide
clumps every three to four years to keep the planting vigorous.
