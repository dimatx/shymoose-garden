*Rudbeckia fulgida* 'Goldblitz' is a sturdy, upright black-eyed Susan that
delivers a long season of golden-yellow daisies with rich brown centers,
starting in midsummer and carrying on well into fall. It's a dependable,
low-fuss performer for sunny borders and cutting gardens alike.

## A workhorse for late-summer color

'Goldblitz' holds its own through summer heat and keeps flowering long after
many perennials have finished for the season. It tolerates average soil,
including clay, and needs little beyond an occasional deadheading to keep the
blooms coming.

## Care tips

Plant in full sun to light shade in well-drained soil. Once established, it
handles dry spells well, though regular watering will extend the bloom
display. Deadhead through the season to encourage more flowers, cut the whole
plant back after frost or in early spring, and divide congested clumps every
few years to keep them vigorous.
