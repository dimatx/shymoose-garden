Arabesque® Red is a striking F1 hybrid penstemon and a 2014 All-America
Selections Flower Award Winner. From early summer on, it produces tall spikes
crowded with large, bell-shaped flowers in a vivid red-and-white bicolor that
hummingbirds and butterflies can't resist.

## A standout in beds and pots

Its upright habit and continuous bloom make it equally at home in a sunny border
or a container on the patio. The big, tubular flowers are perfectly shaped for
hummingbird visits all season.

## Care tips

Grow it in full sun and deadhead spent spikes to keep the flowers coming. With
regular grooming it stays in bloom right through summer.
