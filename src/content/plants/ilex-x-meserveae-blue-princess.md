*Ilex* × *meserveae* 'Blue Princess' is a dense, rounded evergreen holly with glossy, spiny, blue-green leaves on purplish stems. It is one of the hardy "blue hollies" bred by Kathleen Meserve, and it keeps its good looks all winter when most of the garden has gone dormant.

## Berries need a partner

Hollies are dioecious, and 'Blue Princess' is a female clone. Its small greenish-white spring flowers are easy to miss, but once pollinated they ripen into abundant bright red berries that often last into spring. Plant one male such as 'Blue Prince' for every three to five females, within the neighborhood of the female plants. Without a male the shrub is still handsome, just berry-free.

## Uses

Use it as a screen, hedge, foundation planting or evergreen backdrop. The spiny leaves also give birds sheltered cover, and the berries feed them.

## Care tips

Give it average, slightly acidic, moist, well-drained soil with some afternoon shade in hot summers and shelter from cold, drying winter winds. Watch for holly leafminer, spider mites, scale and leaf spots. The berries and leaves are mildly toxic to pets, so site it with that in mind.
