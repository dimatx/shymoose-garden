*Penstemon* 'Dark Towers' is a clump-forming hybrid beardtongue bred at the
University of Nebraska, prized for foliage and stems that hold a deep
burgundy colour all season, capped in early summer by tall, airy spires of
pale pink tubular flowers.

## Taller towers of pale pink

The flower spires rise well above the basal foliage clump, opening loose
panicles of two-lipped, tubular blooms that are perfectly sized for bees and
hummingbirds. Deadheading spent spikes extends the display, and the
ornamental seed heads that follow can be left standing for winter interest.

## Care tips

'Dark Towers' is easy and drought tolerant once established — the main
thing to avoid is wet, poorly drained soil, especially over winter, which
invites root rot. Full sun brings out the darkest foliage colour; light
shade is tolerated but foliage turns more blue-green. Leave the seed heads
through winter, cut old growth back in late winter, and divide clumps every
few years to keep them vigorous. Deer generally avoid it.
