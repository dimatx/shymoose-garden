'Prince Charming' is suited to the front of a shaded border, where its hanging
flowers can be seen rather than hidden behind taller plants. It spreads into
a dense, low colony, contrasting with ferns and larger woodland perennials.

The blue-green foliage remains useful after flowering, then turns gold before
dying back for winter. Watch for slugs around new shoots, and divide only when
you want to move or contain the clump.
