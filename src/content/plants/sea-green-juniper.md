Sea Green Juniper is a compact evergreen with dark green foliage and arching branches that create a fountain-like outline. Plant it in full sun and well-drained soil; once established, it tolerates some drought, but standing water can cause decline.

## Naming and sources

NC State Extension lists this cultivar under *Juniperus chinensis*, while Missouri Botanical Garden places it in *Juniperus × pfitzeriana* and identifies the Pfitzer group as hybrids of *J. chinensis* and *J. sabina*. The latter name is used here.
