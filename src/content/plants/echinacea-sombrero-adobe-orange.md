*Echinacea* Sombrero® Adobe Orange packs an intensely saturated orange onto
a compact, heavily branched coneflower — bred to hold its color and shape
through a long summer season without the flopping that plagues older
coneflower varieties.

## Bred for hardiness and heavier branching

The Sombrero® series improved on earlier coneflower cultivars by breeding in
extra cold hardiness and a much bushier growth habit. Adobe Orange branches
freely from the base, producing far more flowering stems than a typical
seed-grown coneflower, all while standing up to summer heat and dry spells.

## Care tips

Plant in full sun in well-drained soil — it tolerates poor and rocky ground
once established, though it appreciates regular water through its first
season. Deadhead through summer to keep fresh blooms coming, then leave the
seed heads standing through fall and winter as a food source for
goldfinches and other birds before cutting back in early spring.
