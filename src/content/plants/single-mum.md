*Chrysanthemum weyrichii* 'White Bomb' is a species mum, not a florist mum —
a low, spreading perennial that carpets itself in simple white daisies just
as most of the garden is fading for the season.

## A spreading species mum

While florist mums are usually treated as short-lived fall annuals, this
species mum is a genuinely hardy, spreading perennial. It creeps by
rhizomes into a dense mat of dark green foliage, doubling as a
weed-suppressing groundcover for sunny slopes and border edges long before
it flowers.

## Care tips

Plant in full sun in average, well-drained soil; it tolerates a wide range
of soil pH and is drought-tolerant once established, though regular water
keeps it looking its best. Deadhead to prolong the late-season bloom, and
cut back and divide in spring if it spreads further than wanted — its
vigorous, mat-forming habit is a feature, not a flaw, in the right spot.
