*Echinacea purpurea* 'PowWow White' is a compact, seed-grown coneflower cultivar prized for its creamy white, slightly reflexed petals surrounding a golden-orange cone — a striking departure from the magenta-purple of the wild species. Bred by PanAmerican Seed, it shares the species' tough, sun-loving nature while offering a shorter, more uniform habit better suited to smaller borders and containers.

## A brighter take on a garden classic

Where typical purple coneflower forms loose 2–4 ft clumps, 'PowWow White' stays compact at 16–24 inches, with sturdy, well-branched stems that hold up to the wind and rain without staking. Its flowers open in late spring and continue for weeks, with occasional rebloom into fall — all while drawing native bees, butterflies, and skippers, and later feeding goldfinches on the ripened seed heads.

## Care tips

Plant in full sun with fertile, well-drained soil for the strongest bloom, though it tolerates average or poor soils once established. Deadhead spent flowers to extend the bloom season, or leave some seed heads standing through fall and winter for the birds. Cut the dead stems back to 12–24 inches in late winter rather than to the ground — native bees often overwinter in the hollow stubble.
