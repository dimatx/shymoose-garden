*Schizachyrium scoparium* 'Carousel' is a compact selection of little
bluestem, a grass native across the prairies and open woodlands of eastern
and central North America. Its cool blue-green summer foliage shifts to a
fiery copper-orange in fall, topped with fluffy, silvery seedheads that
catch the light and persist well into winter.

## A tidy, four-season native grass

Bred to stay more upright and compact than the wild species, 'Carousel' fits
neatly into smaller gardens without losing the seasonal color shift that
makes little bluestem so valued — cool blue in summer, warm copper in fall,
and silver seedheads standing through the coldest months.

## Care tips

Plant in full sun in average or lean, well-drained soil; it's naturally
adapted to poor, dry, and rocky ground and dislikes soggy conditions. Water
regularly the first season to help it establish, then let it fend for itself
through drought. Leave the foliage and seedheads standing through winter for
structure and wildlife interest, then cut the whole clump back to just a few
inches in late winter before new growth starts.
