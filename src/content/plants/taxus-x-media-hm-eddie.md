*Taxus x media* is a hybrid yew — a cross between English yew (*Taxus
baccata*) and the winter-hardy Japanese yew (*Taxus cuspidata*), first bred
by Massachusetts horticulturist T.D. Hatfield in the early 1900s. 'H.M. Eddie'
is a narrowly columnar selection, forming a dense, upright evergreen shrub
prized as a vertical accent, hedge, or screen.

## A tough, shapeable evergreen

Dark green needle-like foliage emerges light green in spring and holds its
color through winter. The columnar form and fine texture make it a natural
fit for formal hedges, topiary, and foundation plantings, and it is one of
the few conifers that thrives in deep shade as readily as full sun.

## Care tips

Plant in well-drained soil — wet feet are the main threat to this otherwise
tough shrub. It tolerates urban pollution, drought once established, and
hard pruning at almost any time of year, making it an easy, low-maintenance
choice for shaping. Note that all parts of yew are toxic if ingested, so site
it thoughtfully around children and pets.
