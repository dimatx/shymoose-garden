'Bottle Rocket' forms a compact clump of large, toothed green leaves
topped by dense yellow flower spikes on nearly black stems.

## A bold accent for damp shade

Its bold leaves suit pond edges and shaded borders with reliable
moisture. Afternoon heat can cause temporary wilting even in moist soil;
check the soil before adding more water.

## Care tips

Plant in rich, consistently moist soil in part to full shade, sheltered
from drying wind. Watch for slugs and snails and remove spent flower spikes.
