'Shintokiwa' is a vigorous Japanese cucumber that produces long, slender,
thin-skinned fruit. It is prized for staying sweet, crisp, and entirely
bitter-free even when the cucumbers grow large.

## Sweet and crisp

The thin skin means there's no need to peel, and the flavor stays mild and sweet
across a wide range of sizes — a forgiving cucumber for the home garden.

## Care tips

Grow it on a trellis to keep the long fruit straight and clean. For the most
tender texture, pick around 8 inches, but don't worry if a few get away from
you — they hold their sweetness.
