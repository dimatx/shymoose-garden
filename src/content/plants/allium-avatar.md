'Avatar' is a compact ornamental onion with lavender-pink flower clusters
held above narrow green leaves. It flowers after most spring-blooming
alliums, extending their season into summer.

## Care tips

Plant in sun to part shade with well-drained soil. Water regularly during
the first season; established clumps tolerate brief dry spells. Remove
spent flowers to tidy the planting and cut back foliage once it yellows.
