*Hydrangea macrophylla* 'Magical Bride' is a compact selection from the Everlasting® / Hoko™ line, sold under the patent name `'Hortmabrid'` (PP25,579). It brings the classic lush texture of a mophead hydrangea to smaller spaces, with sturdy stems and a neat, rounded habit that works well in a front border, patio container, or bright woodland edge.

## White blooms that mellow to mint green

Plants Nouveau describes the flowers as pure white at first, then gradually tinted mint green as they mature — a trait visible even before the blooms are fully spent. The strong stems also make it a good cut-flower hydrangea. Its reblooming habit on both old and new wood helps extend the display through summer and into early fall.

## Care tips

Species guidance from NC State, Missouri Botanical Garden, and RHS all points to the same core recipe: rich soil, reliable moisture, and protection from harsh afternoon sun. The breeder adds that 'Magical Bride' is somewhat more sun-tolerant than many bigleaf hydrangeas, especially in cooler climates, but it still looks best when the soil never dries out completely. Prune sparingly — mostly deadwood cleanup in spring or light shaping after bloom — so the plant can make the most of buds carried on both old and new growth.
