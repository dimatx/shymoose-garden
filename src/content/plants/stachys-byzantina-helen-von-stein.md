Lamb's ears 'Helen von Stein' is grown primarily for its broad, woolly
silver foliage. The leaves overlap to form a soft-looking mat that brightens
sunny borders and edges, and the cultivar usually produces few or no flower
stalks.

## Silver foliage for sunny edges

This selection spreads by creeping stems and can make an effective low
ground cover in well-drained soil. Its fuzzy leaves are especially
conspicuous in a sunny planting, where they contrast with green foliage and
dark soil.

## Care tips

Give lamb's ears full sun and soil that drains freely. Once established, it
tolerates dry spells; consistently wet soil can cause the foliage and crowns
to rot. Remove damaged leaves as needed and divide the clump if it spreads
beyond its allotted space.
