*Pinus mugo* 'Pumilio' is a dwarf form of the mugo, or Swiss mountain, pine —
a broad, low evergreen that hugs the ground in a dense, spreading mound rather
than growing upright like the species. Dark green needles in bundles of two
give it year-round structure, and its naturally compact habit makes it one of
the most dependable dwarf conifers for small spaces.

## A slow, well-mannered mound

Where the straight species can reach 20 feet, 'Pumilio' grows at a fraction of
that pace, eventually reaching only 3–5 feet tall by 6–10 feet wide — and it
takes many years to get there. That patience makes it a natural fit for
foundation plantings, rock gardens, and the edges of paths, where its texture
and form read as a calm, evergreen anchor beside more colorful perennials.

## Giving it what it needs

Plant it in full sun in loamy or sandy soil with good drainage; it tolerates
clay and urban conditions but resents wet, poorly drained ground. Once
established it is fairly drought tolerant. Little pruning is needed to keep
its shape — an annual late-winter trim, or pinching back the soft spring
candles, is enough to keep it dense and prevent it from spreading beyond its
space.
