'Golden Splendor' resembles the native Canadian (eastern) hemlock in all but
color: its foliage is golden. It was introduced by John Mitsch of Aurora,
Oregon, and per Iseli Nursery it is a fast grower (15+ in. per year) to about
25 ft tall and 15 ft wide, hardy to zone 4.

## Through the seasons

Bright golden-yellow, softly drooping foliage glows year-round. Iseli notes it
tolerates full sun and produces brighter color with more exposure, unlike
other golden cultivars that scorch. Planted in a row, it can be sheared into
a golden hedge.

## Care tips

NC State Extension describes the species as needing moist but well-drained,
somewhat acidic soil in sun to part shade; it grows best where summers are
cool and does not tolerate drought. Hemlock woolly adelgid is a major pest,
along with several scale and mite problems. Sources: Iseli Nursery
(cultivar), NC State Extension Plant Toolbox (species culture).
