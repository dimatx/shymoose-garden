*Achillea millefolium* 'Tutti Frutti' belongs to a series of common-yarrow selections (Takii / Blooms of Bressingham breeding) chosen for vivid, fruit-toned blooms and a sturdy, uniform habit. Like all yarrows it forms a low mat of soft, aromatic, fern-like foliage and sends up sturdy stems carrying broad flat-topped flower heads that bees and butterflies cover all summer.

## Colour that changes by the day

The signature trait of the Tutti Frutti series is its shifting colour. Each large cluster opens one shade — apricot, grapefruit pink, pomegranate or cherry, depending on the cultivar — and gradually matures to another, so the plant looks like a bowl of mixed fruit. The flowers are superb for cutting and dry well for arrangements.

## Care tips

Yarrow is the definition of an easy, drought-tolerant perennial: give it full sun and lean, well-drained soil and otherwise leave it alone. Avoid rich soil and overwatering, which cause floppy growth. Shear the plant back hard after the first flush to keep it tidy and trigger a second round of bloom, deadhead to limit self-seeding, and divide every few years. Deer and rabbits pass it by, while pollinators flock to it. The genus name honours Achilles, who, legend says, used yarrow to staunch his soldiers' battle wounds.
