*Echinacea* Sombrero® 'Fiesta Orange' brings a vivid, sunset-orange twist to
the classic prairie coneflower. Bred as part of Walters Gardens' Sombrero®
series (sold under the variety name 'Balsomstor'), it's heavily branched
compared to older coneflower varieties, producing far more blooms over a
longer season on sturdy, well-behaved stems.

## Bred for more flowers, less flop

The Sombrero® series was developed to improve on older coneflower cultivars
that tend to flop or bloom in a single flush. 'Fiesta Orange' instead branches
freely, keeping fresh orange daisies coming from early summer well into fall,
all while standing up to heat and dry spells better than the wild species.

## Care tips

Plant in full sun in average, well-drained soil — it's tolerant of poor and
rocky ground once established. Water regularly through the first season to
help it root in, after which it handles drought with ease. Deadhead through
summer to keep the blooms coming, then leave the seed heads standing through
fall and winter as a food source for goldfinches and other birds before
cutting back in late winter.
