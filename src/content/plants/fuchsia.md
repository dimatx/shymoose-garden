Hardy fuchsia is a deciduous to semi-evergreen shrub from southern South
America. Its arching stems suit sheltered borders with cool, moist roots.

## Lantern flowers from summer to frost

Scarlet outer sepals frame purple petals and long stamens. The hanging
flowers attract hummingbirds through summer and into fall, followed by
small, dark berries.

## Care tips

Give it fertile, moist, well-drained soil and afternoon shade in hot
climates. Mulch to conserve moisture and protect the roots in winter.
Where frost kills the stems, wait for new basal growth before cutting
back the dead wood in spring. Surviving stems can be pruned in early
spring.
