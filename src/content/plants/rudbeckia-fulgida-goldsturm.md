*Rudbeckia fulgida* 'Goldsturm' is arguably the most recognizable black-eyed
Susan in American gardens — bold, golden-yellow daisies with near-black cones
held on sturdy stems, blooming in dense clumps from midsummer straight through
fall. It's a garden mainstay for good reason: reliable, tough, and covered in
flowers for months.

## A proven, award-winning performer

Few perennials have earned as much recognition as 'Goldsturm.' Its combination
of drought tolerance, long bloom time, and adaptability to average or poor
soils has made it a staple of naturalistic and formal plantings alike, from
highway medians to cottage gardens.

## Care tips

Plant in full sun to light shade in well-drained soil — it tolerates clay and
poor soils without complaint. Water regularly while establishing; afterward
it handles dry spells well. Deadhead to keep the flowers coming, cut the
whole plant back after frost, and divide congested clumps every few years to
maintain vigor.
