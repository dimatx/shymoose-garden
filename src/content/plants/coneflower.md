*Echinacea* Summersong™ Firefinch™ brings a warm reddish-orange to the
classic prairie coneflower, on a compact plant bred for heavier branching
than older varieties. That extra branching means far more flowers over a
longer season, from early summer clear through to frost.

## Bred for bold color and more blooms

The Summersong® series was one of the first breeding programs to expand
coneflower color beyond the usual pinks, purples, and whites into true reds
and oranges. Firefinch keeps that vivid color coming non-stop thanks to its
branching habit, which produces many more flowering stems than a typical
seed-grown coneflower.

## Care tips

Plant in full sun in average, well-drained soil — it tolerates poor and
rocky ground once established. Water regularly through the first season to
help it root in, after which it shrugs off drought with ease. Deadhead
through summer to keep new blooms coming, then leave the seed heads standing
through fall and winter as a food source for goldfinches and other birds
before cutting back in late winter or early spring.
