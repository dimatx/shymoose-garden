*Schizachyrium scoparium* 'Standing Ovation' is a selection of little bluestem, a warm-season bunchgrass native to prairies and open woodlands across most of North America. It brings fine-textured, upright structure to sunny borders and naturalistic plantings, with vivid seasonal color changes from blue-green summer foliage to blazing orange-red fall color.

## Built to stand tall

The straight species of little bluestem is beloved for its color but notorious for flopping open in late summer, especially in richer garden soil. 'Standing Ovation' was selected specifically for a stiffer, more upright clump that holds its shape through the season. In late summer, airy purplish-bronze flower clusters appear atop the foliage, maturing into fluffy silvery-white seed heads that catch the low fall and winter light and persist well into the cold months.

## Care tips

Little bluestem thrives in full sun and average-to-dry, well-drained soil — it's at its best in lean conditions and can flop or lodge if overfed or overwatered. Once established it's exceptionally drought tolerant and needs no supplemental water. The only regular maintenance is an annual cutback to a few inches above the ground in late winter or very early spring, before new blades emerge. Left standing through winter, its seed heads provide food and cover for birds.
