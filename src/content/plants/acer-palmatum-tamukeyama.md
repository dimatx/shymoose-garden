*Acer palmatum* 'Tamukeyama' is a weeping laceleaf (dissectum) Japanese maple
grown for combining vigor and toughness with the delicate grace of a cutleaf
maple. Its deeply dissected, thread-like leaves emerge and hold a deep
crimson-purple through the growing season — resisting the mid-summer fade to
green that troubles many red laceleafs — before finishing bright scarlet in
autumn.

## Built for heat

Among red cutleaf maples, 'Tamukeyama' is one of the most heat- and
humidity-tolerant, which has made it a favorite far beyond its cool-climate
cousins. It grows more vigorously than many laceleafs, its branches arching over
into a broad, cascading mound that typically reaches 6–8 feet tall and 8–12 feet
wide over many years.

## A specimen for any garden

Its weeping, mushroom-like form makes it a natural focal point — near a patio,
at the edge of a border, in a rock garden, or beside a pond where the cascading
branches can reflect in the water. It layers beautifully as an understory tree
beneath taller canopy that provides the dappled shade it prefers, and it is
generally left alone by deer.
