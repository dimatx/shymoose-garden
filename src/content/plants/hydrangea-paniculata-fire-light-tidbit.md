Fire Light Tidbit is a dwarf selection of panicle hydrangea, a species native
to eastern Asia that is one of the hardiest and most sun-tolerant hydrangeas.
Where the typical species is a rangy 8–15 ft shrub, this cultivar stays a
neat, sturdy-stemmed mound of just 2–3 ft, which makes it suitable for small
beds, borders and containers.

## Through the seasons

Large, full, mophead-shaped panicles of white flowers open in early to mid
summer, nearly hiding the textured green leaves. As summer progresses they
take on bright pink and red tones that carry the plant right up to frost, and
the foliage itself colors in autumn — an unusual trait among panicle
hydrangeas.

## Care tips

Plant in full to part sun in average, well-drained soil and mulch the
shallow root zone. Because it flowers on new wood, a hard winter or a late
frost will not cost you the blooms; cut it back by about a third in late
winter or early spring if you want to shape it. Reviews on the breeder's
site are mixed on winter survival in colder zones, so a protected site and
consistent moisture while it establishes are worthwhile.

> **Heads up:** Hydrangeas contain cyanogenic glycosides and are listed by
> the ASPCA as toxic to dogs and cats. Sources: breeder page (Proven
> Winners), NC State Extension Plant Toolbox (species culture), ASPCA
> toxic-plant database.
