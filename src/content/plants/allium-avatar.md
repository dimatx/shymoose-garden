*Allium tanguticum* 'Avatar' is a patented ornamental onion valued for its
long season of rounded, lavender-pink flower clusters held above clumps of
narrow, grass-like foliage. It blooms reliably from mid-summer into early
fall, well after most spring-flowering alliums have finished.

## A bud sport with staying power

'Avatar' arose as a bud sport of the popular 'Summer Beauty' allium — a
spontaneous genetic variant discovered growing on an otherwise typical
plant. Once its distinct, more compact habit proved stable through
propagation, it was released and patented in its own right. The two
cultivars share the same easy care and long bloom time.

## Care tips

Plant in full sun to partial shade in average, well-drained soil. Water
regularly the first season to help it settle in, then it tolerates brief dry
spells without complaint. Deadhead spent flower clusters through the season
to keep the planting tidy, and cut the foliage back once it yellows in fall.
