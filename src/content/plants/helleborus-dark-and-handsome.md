Hellebore 'Dark and Handsome' is a compact evergreen perennial grown for
its unusually dark, double flowers. Rounded, leathery foliage forms a
year-round mound, and flower stems rise above it in late winter and early
spring.

## Dark flowers in the winter garden

The deep burgundy blooms can appear almost black, especially in shade, while
their yellow-green centers bring out the detail of each flower. The long
flowering season and persistent foliage make this hybrid a strong choice for
a sheltered border or woodland edge.

## Care tips

Grow it in part shade with rich, well-drained soil and moderate moisture.
Avoid places where water stands around the crown. Before buds open, remove
old or damaged leaves to display the flowers; cut spent stems back after
blooming.

> **Heads up:** all parts of hellebores are toxic if eaten, especially the
> roots. Keep the plant away from curious pets and children.
