*Pennisetum alopecuroides* 'Hameln' is a compact selection of Chinese fountain grass, native to open grasslands and meadows across East Asia and Australia. It forms a tidy, rounded mound of fine arching blades — smaller in every dimension than the straight species — topped from late summer into fall by silvery-tan, foxtail-shaped flower plumes that arch outward in a true fountain shape.

## A compact fountain grass

'Hameln' blooms earlier and stays smaller than the species, making it
useful in borders and containers. Do not assume it is sterile: remove
unwanted seedlings where self-seeding occurs.

## Care tips

Give it full sun and well-drained soil for the densest form and best bloom; it tolerates a wide pH range and both dry and occasionally wet conditions once established. Cut the entire clump back to the ground in late winter before new growth emerges — leaving the tan foliage standing through winter provides texture and habitat in the meantime. Divide congested clumps in spring if needed, though 'Hameln' is slow to outgrow its space.
