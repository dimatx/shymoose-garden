*Cercis canadensis* Flame Thrower® is a redbud cultivar bred by Dennis J. Werner
at North Carolina State University, created by crossing 'The Rising Sun' (a
non-weeping, gold-leaf redbud) with a weeping, purple-leaf seedling known as
'NC2006-14'. The resulting selection, 'NC2016-2', was introduced commercially
as Flame Thrower.

## A tree that's always changing color

Like its native parent species, Flame Thrower opens the spring with clusters of
rosy pink-purple, pea-like flowers along its bare branches, before any leaves
appear. But its signature feature comes after bloom: each heart-shaped leaf
emerges deep purple, quickly shifts to red as it expands, then continues aging
through reddish-bronze and finally yellow-green. Because leaves at every stage
of that transition are present on the tree simultaneously, the whole canopy
displays a shifting, multicolor mix of purple, red, orange, and gold —
especially during flushes of active growth in spring and early summer. The
effect fades a bit as summer heat sets in after mid-July.

## Care tips

Give Flame Thrower full sun to part shade — full sun brings out the most vivid
leaf color — and well-drained soil of average fertility across a range of pH.
It shows a gentle pendant habit but isn't a true weeper, reaching 15–20 ft tall
and 10–15 ft wide. Like all redbuds, it resents being transplanted once
established, so pick its permanent spot carefully. Prune only lightly, just
after flowering, to shape the tree or remove dead and crossing wood.
