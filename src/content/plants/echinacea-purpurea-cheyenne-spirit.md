*Echinacea purpurea* 'Cheyenne Spirit' takes the toughness of the native purple
coneflower and turns it into a color festival. Instead of blooming in one fixed
shade, each seed-grown planting opens in a shifting mix of red, orange, yellow,
cream, pink, and purple, all on sturdy, well-branched plants.

What makes it especially useful is that it flowers in its first year from seed
instead of making gardeners wait a second season for the show. Butterflies and
other pollinators work the blooms all summer, and if a few seed heads are left
standing, goldfinches and other birds will visit them later on.

Treat it like a classic coneflower: plenty of sun, good drainage, and only
moderate watering once established. It tolerates heat, humidity, and ordinary
garden soils well, but it dislikes sitting wet in winter. Deadhead if you want
the longest display, or leave some of the cones in place for fall and winter
interest.
