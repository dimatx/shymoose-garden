*Geum* TEMPO™ 'Orange' is a compact, clump-forming avens bred by Terra Nova
Nurseries, producing double to single ruffled flowers in warm
cream-peach-orange shades over a low, tidy mound of green foliage — the
smallest and lowest-growing of the TEMPO™ series.

## A low, warm-toned bloomer

Flowering begins in early spring and can carry into early summer, with the
compact scale keeping every bloom held well above the foliage rather than
sprawling. Bees and other pollinators visit readily.

## Care tips

Grow in full sun to part shade in moist, loamy, well-drained soil, watering
at an average rate and letting the soil dry somewhat between waterings.
Deadhead to prolong bloom, feed with a balanced fertilizer each spring
after the first year, and cut the plant back in fall ahead of winter. It is
low maintenance and generally left alone by deer, making it an easy, early
splash of color at the front of a border.
