Foamflower (*Tiarella cordifolia*) is a native woodland perennial. The
'Brandywine' selection is especially vigorous, with glossy, textured leaves
that form a compact clump and may spread on short runners.

## Flowers and foliage

In spring, slender stems carry creamy white flower spikes above the foliage.
The flowers can last for six to eight weeks, while the scalloped leaves keep
their texture and develop bronze tones in fall and winter.

## Care tips

Grow foamflower in dappled to full shade in humus-rich soil that stays moist
but drains well. It dislikes drought and should not sit in saturated soil
through winter. Divide established clumps or separate rooted plantlets to
propagate it.
