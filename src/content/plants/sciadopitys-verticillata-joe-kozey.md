'Joe Kozey' is a narrow, upright selection of Japanese umbrella pine,
introduced by Sidney Waxman at the University of Connecticut. At 20 ft
tall it is only about 6 ft wide, with
sturdy branches held tight to the trunk that can withstand heavy snow loads.

## Through the seasons

It is evergreen: glossy green needles in whorls give a year-round, spire-like
silhouette that grows slowly into a large tree. Reddish-brown bark that
exfoliates in strips adds interest on older trees.

## Care tips

Plant in rich, moist, well-drained, slightly acidic soil in sun or part
shade, sheltered from harsh winter wind. Keep the soil consistently moist;
it does best in cool summers and appreciates afternoon shade in hot climates.
