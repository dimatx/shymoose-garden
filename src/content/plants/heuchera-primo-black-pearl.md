*Heuchera* Primo® 'Black Pearl' is a coral bells grown above all for its
foliage: a dense, weather-resistant mound of large, glossy leaves with
scalloped, ruffled edges. The tops are a near-true jet black, while the
undersides flush rosy purple — a combination that reads as inky and dramatic in
the border, in a container, or massed along a shaded path.

Unusually adaptable for a dark-leaved heuchera, it holds up in everything from
full sun to full shade, keeping its color best where it gets a little light. In
midsummer it sends up airy sprays of small white flowers on slender stems that
hover over the foliage and pull in bees, butterflies, and hummingbirds. Give it
humus-rich, well-drained soil, tidy the foliage in early spring, and it will
return reliably year after year.
