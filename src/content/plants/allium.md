Allium 'Big Beauty' is an ornamental onion grown purely for its flowers —
tall, leafless stems topped with an oversized globe of small, star-shaped
pink-purple blossoms that hold their shape for weeks.

## Big blooms on sturdy stems

Bred from the giant Central Asian onion species that give garden alliums
their towering flower heads, 'Big Beauty' was selected for flower globes up
to 6 inches across on stems sturdy enough not to flop under their own
weight — a common complaint with older giant allium varieties.

## Care tips

Plant in full sun in well-drained soil; alliums are unfussy about soil type
as long as it doesn't stay waterlogged. Water regularly while establishing,
then let it dry between waterings — like most alliums, it's quite
drought-tolerant once settled in. The strappy foliage often yellows and
fades before or during bloom, so plant companions nearby to hide it. Cut
back the flower stalks once the blooms fade, or leave the dried seed heads
for winter texture.
