*Ligularia dentata* 'Othello' is a bold-leaved perennial from China and Japan.
It forms a broad clump of rounded leaves, with reddish-purple undersides,
veins, and stems that make a striking contrast in a shady, moisture-rich
garden.

## Foliage and flowers

New foliage emerges purplish red and matures to brownish green above with
purple undersides. In July and August, tall stems carry loose clusters of
yellow-orange, daisy-like flowers above the leaves.

## Care tips

Give 'Othello' humusy soil that stays moist to wet and a sheltered spot in
part to full shade. It wilts in hot sun and can suffer if the soil dries out,
so water deeply during hot or dry spells. Remove spent flower stalks and cut
back old foliage after frost.
