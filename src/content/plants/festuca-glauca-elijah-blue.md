*Festuca glauca* 'Elijah Blue' is a compact ornamental grass grown for its
intensely silvery-blue, needle-fine foliage, which forms neat, tidy mounds
year-round. It's a favorite for edging paths and borders, or as a repeating
accent in a sunny bed, adding cool color and fine texture with very little
fuss.

## A tough, low-maintenance grass

'Elijah Blue' thrives on neglect once established, tolerating heat, drought,
and lean soil without complaint. Its main requirement is good drainage —
soggy, heavy clay is the surest way to lose it, especially over winter.

## Care tips

Plant in full sun in average or lean, well-drained soil for the most vivid
blue foliage color. Water regularly the first season, then let established
plants ride out dry spells on their own. In early spring, shear the whole
plant back by a third to two-thirds to remove tired old growth before fresh
blue blades emerge, and divide any clumps that die out in the center.
