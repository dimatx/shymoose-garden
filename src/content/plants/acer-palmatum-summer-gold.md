'Summer Gold' provides a light-colored focal point beside darker foliage.
The leaves brighten toward gold through summer, then develop yellow, orange,
and red tones in autumn.

Sun tolerance does not remove the need for reliable moisture and wind shelter.
In an exposed or hot garden, favor afternoon shade over the strongest possible
yellow leaf color.
