Feather reed grass 'Karl Foerster' is a tidy, upright clump-forming grass.
Its narrow green leaves make a vertical accent in the border, followed by
slender, feathery plumes that catch the light from early summer into winter.

## Upright summer plumes

The flower stems rise above the foliage in early summer, opening pink-purple
before drying to a warm straw color. Unlike many ornamental grasses, the
stems stay upright after flowering, adding texture to the garden through
fall and winter. This cultivar is sterile and will not spread by seed.

## Care tips

Grow it in full sun for the strongest flowering, though it tolerates some
shade. It adapts to a range of ordinary garden soils, including clay, and
handles both moist conditions and some drought once established. In late
winter or early spring, cut back the previous year's stems before new growth
starts.
