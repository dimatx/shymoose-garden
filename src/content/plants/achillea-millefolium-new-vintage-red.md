*Achillea millefolium* New Vintage™ Red is a compact selection of common yarrow grown for eye-catching crimson-red flower heads on a short, dense, well-mannered plant. Like all yarrows it forms a low mat of soft, aromatic, fern-like foliage, but the flowering stems stay markedly shorter than the species — a deliberate breeding goal that keeps the flat flower clusters from flopping.

## Long-blooming jewel tones

The flowers open a deep, saturated red and hold their colour better than many older red yarrows, which tend to fade toward pink. Bloom begins in late spring and continues through summer, and shearing spent heads usually brings a fresh round into autumn. The compact habit makes it useful at the front of a border, tucked among taller perennials, or in containers.

## Care tips

This is a tough, drought-tolerant, sun-loving perennial. Plant it in lean, well-drained soil and resist the urge to feed or overwater — rich, wet ground makes yarrow leggy. Deadhead or shear after the main flush to rebloom and to limit self-seeding, cut the plant down in late winter, and divide every couple of years. Bees and butterflies love the flat landing-pad flowers, deer and rabbits leave it alone, and the blooms are excellent fresh or dried for the vase.
