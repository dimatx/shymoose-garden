Flame Thrower® is an eastern redbud grown for its multicolored foliage
and rosy spring flowers. Its gently drooping branches form a small tree
rather than a true weeping mound.

## A tree that's always changing color

Pink-purple flowers open on bare branches before the heart-shaped leaves.
New foliage emerges purple and changes through red and bronze to
yellow-green. The mix is most vivid during spring and early-summer growth,
then softens as summer progresses.

## Care tips

Give it sun to part shade and well-drained soil; more sun improves leaf
color. Choose a permanent spot, since established redbuds transplant
poorly. Shape lightly just after flowering.
