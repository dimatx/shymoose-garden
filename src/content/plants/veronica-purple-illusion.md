*Veronica* Magic Show® 'Purple Illusion' is a compact spiked speedwell that
packs an enormous number of upright, densely flowered spikes onto a tidy,
rounded mound — the effect from a distance is almost a solid block of rich
purple. Glossy, dark green foliage forms a full canopy that keeps the plant
looking good even between flushes of bloom.

## A long season of purple spikes

Flowering runs from early into midsummer, and because 'Purple Illusion' was
bred for a strong repeat bloom, shearing the spent spikes after the first
flush reliably triggers a second round later in the season. Bees and
butterflies work the flowers heavily throughout.

## Care tips

Give it full sun and average, well-drained soil enriched with compost for the
best performance; it tolerates a bit of shade but blooms less densely. Water
regularly but avoid wetting the foliage, since good airflow and drier leaves
help keep powdery mildew at bay. Shear after the first bloom, cut the whole
plant back in late fall or early spring, and divide every few years to keep
clumps vigorous. Deer generally leave it alone.
