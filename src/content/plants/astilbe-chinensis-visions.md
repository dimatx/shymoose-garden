*Astilbe chinensis* 'Visions' brings dense, upright plumes of raspberry-pink to
purple flowers over finely cut, deep green foliage — a compact astilbe that
fits neatly into smaller shade gardens or the front of a border. It's also
one of the few fragrant astilbe selections, adding a light scent to its
summer display.

## Compact and reliably moist-loving

'Visions' stays tidier than many astilbes, making it a good choice where space
is limited. Like the rest of the genus, though, it needs steady moisture —
plant it in rich, humus-rich soil and keep it from drying out, especially
during its summer bloom period.

## Care tips

Site in partial to full shade with consistently moist, well-drained soil.
Mulch helps conserve moisture through dry spells. Deadhead or leave the spent
plumes standing for winter interest, and divide clumps every two to three
years in early spring to keep the planting vigorous.
