*Tsuga canadensis* 'Moon Frost' is a dwarf Canadian hemlock selected for its
bright, glowing new growth — needles emerge nearly white in spring before
mellowing to a soft green, giving established plants a two-tone, frosted look
that's especially striking against darker evergreens. Young plants form a
tidy, compact globe that gradually broadens into a looser mound with age.

## A patient, shade-loving dwarf

Like most dwarf conifer selections, 'Moon Frost' grows at a glacial pace — just
a few inches a year — so it stays garden-scaled for decades. It prefers a
sheltered spot with partial shade, protected from strong wind and the hottest
afternoon sun, which can scorch its pale new growth. Give it consistently moist,
acidic, well-drained soil and a layer of mulch, since it does not tolerate
drought the way many conifers do.

## Care tips

Plant where afternoon shade is available, especially in warmer climates, and
keep the soil evenly moist rather than letting it dry out. Pruning is rarely
needed; if you do want to tidy the shape, do it in late May or June. Avoid
roadside or urban sites with heavy pollution, which this species tolerates
poorly, and keep an eye out for hemlock woolly adelgid, a serious regional pest
of hemlocks in the eastern U.S.
