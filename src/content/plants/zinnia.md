*Zinnia elegans* 'Benary's Giant Lilac' is a tall, vigorous cultivar of the common zinnia, an annual in the aster family (Asteraceae) with origins in Mexico. The Benary's Giant series was bred specifically for the cut-flower trade and produces blooms up to 4–5 inches across on straight, sturdy stems that hold up beautifully in vases.

## A summer-to-frost spectacle

Plants grow 3–4 feet tall with an upright habit, and once they begin flowering in early summer they do not stop until frost kills them. The fully double blooms in soft lilac-purple are produced in profusion above rough, opposite leaves. Butterflies, bees, and hummingbirds visit constantly. Deadheading spent flowers is the most important task — it diverts energy to new buds and keeps the display going for months.

## Care tips

Zinnias are among the easiest annuals to grow. Direct-sow seeds in a sunny spot after the last frost date; they germinate quickly in warm soil and resent root disturbance, so transplanting is less successful than direct seeding. Water at the base rather than overhead — good air circulation and dry foliage dramatically reduce powdery mildew, which is the only common problem. In humid climates, spacing plants widely and choosing disease-resistant cultivars helps. Cut stems freely all summer; the more you cut, the more the plant flowers.
