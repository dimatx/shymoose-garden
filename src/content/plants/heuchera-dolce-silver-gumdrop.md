*Heuchera* Dolce® 'Silver Gumdrop' is a compact coral bells made for brightening
the shade. Its semi-glossy leaves shimmer an iridescent silver, then warm to a
rosy blush as the season goes on, so the plant shifts gently in color from
spring through fall. The neat, mounded habit makes it an easy fit at the front
of a border, along a path, or tucked into a container.

In midsummer it lifts slender scapes of vibrant pink flowers above the silvery
foliage — a cheerful contrast that bees, butterflies, and hummingbirds are quick
to find. Like other heucheras it asks only for humus-rich, well-drained soil and
a light tidy-up of worn leaves in early spring, after which it returns
faithfully year after year.
