Fire Light Tidbit is a dwarf panicle hydrangea with sturdy stems and a
rounded habit suited to small beds, borders, and containers.

## Through the seasons

Full white flower clusters open in summer and develop pink and red tones
as they age. Fall foliage adds color after the main flower display.

## Care tips

Plant in full to part sun with well-drained soil and consistent moisture.
Mulch the shallow roots. It flowers on new growth; shorten stems by about
one-third in late winter or early spring if shaping is needed.
