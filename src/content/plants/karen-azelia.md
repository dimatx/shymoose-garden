'Karens' is a cold-hardy evergreen azalea with a dense habit, lavender-pink
spring flowers, and reddish winter foliage.

## A spring showstopper

Lavender-pink flowers cover the shrub in mid-spring. Small, glossy green
leaves provide summer structure before taking on burgundy tones as the
weather cools.

## Care tips

Plant in part shade with acidic, humus-rich, well-drained soil. Keep the
shallow roots moist but not waterlogged, and mulch without burying the
base. Shelter from harsh winter winds. Trim lightly just after flowering;
later pruning removes next spring's buds.
