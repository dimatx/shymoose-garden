*Gaillardia* x grandiflora 'Goblin' is a dwarf blanket flower bred for a
tidy, ground-hugging habit and nonstop red-and-yellow daisies from early
summer clear through to fall — all on a plant that thrives on neglect.

## Bred to be tough and tidy

Blanket flowers are native to the harsh, sun-baked prairies of central and
southern North America, and 'Goblin' inherits that toughness in a much more
compact package than the wild species. Its low mounding habit and long
bloom season have earned it a long-standing RHS Award of Garden Merit.

## Care tips

Plant in full sun in average, well-drained soil — it thrives in sandy or
rocky spots and needs little supplemental water once established. Good
drainage matters more than fertility; heavy, wet clay is its main enemy,
especially over winter. Deadhead through the season to keep the blooms
coming, cut the clump back by about half in late summer to help it survive
winter, and divide every couple of years to keep it vigorous.
