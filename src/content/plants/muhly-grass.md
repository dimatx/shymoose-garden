*Muhlenbergia reverchonii* 'Undaunted' is a selected form of seep muhly, a
warm-season bunchgrass native to rocky ground in Oklahoma and Texas. It forms a
dark green, fine-textured fountain of narrow leaves, then steals the show in
late summer and fall when the entire plant softens into a hazy pink cloud of
flowers.

This cultivar is prized because it combines the airy beauty gardeners love in
muhly grasses with better cold hardiness than many other pink-flowering species.
The plumes open rosy pink to ruby pink, then fade to tawny tones that still
look beautiful through winter. Plant it where low autumn light can backlight
the bloom for the full smoky, glowing effect.

Give 'Undaunted' full sun and excellent drainage, especially in winter. Lean,
rocky, or sandy soil suits it just fine, and once rooted in it is very drought
tolerant. Heavy, soggy soil is the real enemy. Leave the clump standing
through winter for texture, then shear it down just before spring growth starts
again.
