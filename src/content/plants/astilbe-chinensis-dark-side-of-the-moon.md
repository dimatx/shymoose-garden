*Astilbe chinensis* 'Dark Side of the Moon' pairs feathery, dusky pink-purple
flower plumes with strikingly dark, glossy foliage — a rare astilbe selection
where the leaves are as much of a draw as the bloom. It's a reliable choice for
adding texture and season-long color to the shady, damp spots where many other
perennials struggle.

## A thirsty shade lover

Like all astilbes, this hybrid wants consistent moisture. It's not a plant to
tuck into a dry shade bed and forget — give it rich, humus-heavy soil that
stays evenly damp, and it will reward you with dense plumes over near-black
foliage from mid- to late summer. A layer of mulch helps retain moisture
through hot spells.

## Care tips

Plant in partial to full shade with some morning sun if the soil can keep up.
Water deeply during dry stretches rather than letting the soil dry out between
waterings. Cut back spent flower stalks in late winter or early spring, and
divide congested clumps every three to four years to keep them vigorous.
