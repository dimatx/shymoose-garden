*Heuchera* Primo® 'Pistachio Ambrosia' is a big, bold coral bells whose large,
well-rounded leaves glow a fresh chartreuse — a color that lights up shady
corners where darker foliage would simply disappear. The leaves take on warmer
amber and pink tones as the seasons turn, giving it interest from spring well
into fall.

It is one of the largest heucheras available, building a broad mound that can
spread nearly three feet across, so give it room to fill out. Color is best in
filtered shade or with a little morning sun; protect it from hot afternoon light,
which can scorch the bright leaves. In midsummer tall stems of soft pink flowers
rise above the foliage for the bees, butterflies, and hummingbirds. Plant in
rich, well-drained soil, tidy it in early spring, and it returns dependably each
year.
