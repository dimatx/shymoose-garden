Single mum 'Sheffield Pink' is a hardy garden chrysanthemum with a
long-running display of simple, daisy-like flowers. Its soft apricot-pink
petals and golden centers arrive as many summer blooms are fading.

## A late-season flower

This clump-forming perennial grows into a leafy mound about as wide as it is
tall. From late summer through frost, its flowers provide color when the
garden is winding down and attract butterflies.

## Care tips

Plant it in full sun and fertile, well-drained soil that stays evenly moist.
Pinch the stems from late spring through midsummer to encourage branching
and a compact habit. After flowering, cut the plant back and add winter
mulch where cold conditions threaten the crown.

> **Heads up:** chrysanthemums are toxic to dogs if eaten and can also cause
> skin irritation. Keep the plant away from pets that chew garden plants.
