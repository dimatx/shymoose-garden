Bigleaf hydrangea is a deciduous shrub with broad, glossy leaves and
large summer flower clusters. Mophead forms have rounded heads; lacecaps
have flatter clusters with showy flowers around the edge.

## Flowers that change color

Many cultivars flower blue or purple in acidic soil and pink in alkaline
soil, depending on aluminum availability. White cultivars stay white.
The flowerheads can be used fresh or dried.

## Care tips

Provide morning sun, afternoon shade, and moist, well-drained soil.
Mulch to protect shallow roots. Most forms bloom on old wood: shape just
after flowering, not in winter or spring. Cold can damage overwintering
buds; reblooming cultivars also flower on new growth.
