*Caryopteris* × *clandonensis* Beyond Midnight® is a compact, mounding bluebeard with glossy dark green leaves and spikes of deep blue flowers that arrive in late summer and last into fall. It fills a gap when much of the garden is fading and is a reliable magnet for bees and butterflies.

## An easy sun-lover

Give it full sun and loose, well-drained soil. Once established it is drought tolerant, pest and disease resistant, and rarely bothered by deer. The foliage is aromatic when brushed. Use it in groups, in borders, or as a low informal hedge.

## Care tips

Flowers form on new wood, so cut the plant back hard in late winter to early spring, leaving a few inches of stem. In colder zones the stems may die back to the ground over winter, which is normal. Avoid wet soil and heavy fertilizer. Cultivar size and hardiness follow Proven Winners, with genus culture from NC State Extension.
