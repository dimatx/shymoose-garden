'Shindeshojo' is a small, shrubby maple rather than a large shade tree.
Its strongest red display comes with the new spring leaves, so summer-green
foliage is normal rather than a sign that the cultivar has been lost.

Use it as a specimen near a woodland edge or in a sheltered mixed border.
Give it room to spread naturally and avoid hot, dry sites.
