*Coreopsis verticillata* 'Crazy Cayenne' is a threadleaf coreopsis grown for
its fine, ferny foliage and its abundant, brightly-colored flowers — a
striking orange-red that stands out among the softer yellows typical of the
genus. It blooms generously from early summer well into fall.

## Fine foliage, fiery color

The narrow, thread-like leaves give this cultivar a light, airy texture that
works beautifully massed in a border or mixed with bolder perennials. Against
that fine foliage, the daisy-like orange-red flowers practically glow, and
they keep coming for months rather than fading after a single early-summer
flush.

## Care tips

Plant in full sun in average to lean, well-drained soil — it's naturally
tolerant of poor and sandy ground and dislikes wet feet. Water regularly the
first season to help it establish, then it shrugs off drought on its own.
Shear the plant back by about a third after its first big flush of bloom to
tidy it up and coax out a strong second round, then cut it to the ground in
late winter before new growth emerges.
