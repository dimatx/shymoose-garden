'Blagon', also sold as Goldspire or Fastigiata Blagon, forms a slender vertical
accent with ascending branches. It is a male selection, avoiding the usual
mess and odor of seeds produced by female ginkgos.

Plan for long-term height even when the young tree appears compact. Keep it
away from overhead wires and give the roots uncompacted growing space rather
than treating the narrow canopy as permission to squeeze it against a building.
