'Filicoides' is a distinctive Hinoki cypress cultivar prized for its congested,
fern-like sprays of foliage — its name literally means "fern-like." The dense,
pendant branchlets give it a texture unlike almost any other conifer, on a
slow-growing, narrowly pyramidal frame.

## A slow, textural conifer

Where the straight species can eventually tower 50–75 feet, 'Filicoides' stays
compact — typically 8–12 feet tall and just 3–4 feet wide even after many
years — making it a good fit for foundation plantings or as a specimen where a
tall, narrow evergreen accent is wanted without eventually overwhelming the
space.

## Care tips

Give it moist, well-drained, slightly acidic soil and full sun to part shade,
with some shelter from harsh winter wind. It needs little pruning beyond light
shaping, and — like other false cypress — won't resprout from bare wood, so
avoid cutting back into leafless branches. Watch for bagworms, root rot, and
juniper blight.
