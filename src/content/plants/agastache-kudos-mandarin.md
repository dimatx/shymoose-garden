*Agastache* 'Kudos Mandarin' is a compact hummingbird mint bred by Terra
Nova Nurseries, forming a tidy mound of aromatic, minty-licorice-scented
foliage topped from early summer through fall by dense, upright spikes of
vivid orange, tubular flowers.

## A long season of orange spikes

The flower spikes open continuously over an unusually long season, from
early summer well into fall, drawing hummingbirds, bees, and butterflies
the entire time. Deadheading spent spikes keeps new ones coming.

## Care tips

Give it full sun and well-drained soil — it tolerates a wide range of soil
types as long as drainage is good, and it's drought tolerant once
established, so avoid overwatering or letting it sit in soggy ground,
especially over winter. Good airflow helps prevent powdery mildew. Trim old
stems back in spring to refresh the plant, and enjoy the fragrant foliage
and steady stream of pollinators all summer and fall. Deer and rabbits
generally leave it alone.
