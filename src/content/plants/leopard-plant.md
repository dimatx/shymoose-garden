*Ligularia* 'Bottle Rocket' is a patented, compact selection prized for its dense clumps of large, dark green, serrated leaves and its striking flower display: tight, upright spikes of mustard-yellow blooms held on nearly black stems, standing just above the foliage like a bouquet of tiny rockets. It's a sport of the taller 'Little Rocket', discovered in the growing fields at Walters Gardens in 2006.

## A bold accent for damp shade

Like all Ligularia, 'Bottle Rocket' wants constantly moist, rich soil and a sheltered spot in part to full shade — hot midday sun or drying wind will cause even well-watered plants to wilt dramatically by afternoon (though they usually recover by evening). Its bold, tropical-looking foliage and colorful spikes make it a natural fit for pond edges, streamsides, rain gardens, and shaded borders where moisture is reliable.

## Care tips

Plant in rich, humusy soil that stays evenly moist — never let it dry out — and give it protection from strong sun and wind. Watch for slugs and snails, which can significantly damage the foliage; deer generally leave it alone. Divide clumps in spring or right after flowering to maintain vigor, and cut back the spent flower spikes once blooms fade.
