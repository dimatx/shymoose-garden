'Inaba-shidare' is a vigorous laceleaf Japanese maple. Upright branches
arch into a broad, cascading mound that can eventually reach 10 feet tall
and 12 feet or more across.

## Foliage that holds its color

Feathery leaves emerge deep purple, remain purple-red through summer,
and turn bright red in autumn. Its sweeping form makes a focal point
near a patio, pond, or woodland edge.

## Care tips

Plant in moist, slightly acidic, well-drained soil and mulch the shallow
roots. Give afternoon shade in hot climates and shelter from drying
winds. Keep pruning light and do it during dormancy.
