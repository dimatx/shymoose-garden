'Crimson Queen' is a slow-growing, weeping Japanese maple for small gardens,
courtyards, and containers. Its arching branches form a broad mound.

## Stunning foliage all season long

Finely cut leaves hold their crimson-red color through summer, then turn
scarlet in fall. Bare winter branches reveal the tree's cascading form.

## Care tips

Provide dappled sun, moist, well-drained soil, and shelter from hot
afternoon sun and drying winds. Mulch the shallow roots and avoid heavy
pruning; its natural form needs little shaping.
