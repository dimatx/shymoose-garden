*Geum* TEMPO™ 'Yellow' is a compact, clump-forming avens bred by Terra Nova
Nurseries, carrying semi-double yellow flowers with fine dark edging on
near-black, wiry stems above a tidy mound of green foliage.

## Bred to stay tidy

Older geum varieties are notorious for sprawling and flopping once in
bloom; the TEMPO™ series was specifically selected to keep a neat, mounding
habit instead. 'Yellow' blooms in early spring and can continue into early
summer, drawing bees and butterflies throughout.

## Care tips

Plant in full sun to part shade in moist, loamy, well-drained soil, and
water at an average rate, letting the soil dry out somewhat between
waterings. Deadhead spent blooms to keep the display going, feed with a
balanced fertilizer each spring after the first year, and cut the plant
back in fall to help it overwinter cleanly. It is low maintenance, deer
resistant, and a reliable early-season draw for pollinators.
