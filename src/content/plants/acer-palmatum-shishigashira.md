'Shishigashira', also called lion's-head maple, brings a dense, sculptural
texture to a sheltered border or woodland edge. The curled leaves are a
normal cultivar trait, not a reason to prune off healthy shoots.

Young trees can look sparsely furnished before the crown fills out. Give
this slow grower room for its eventual width rather than treating it as a
permanently miniature tree.
