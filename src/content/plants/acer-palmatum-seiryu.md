'Seiryu' brings fine texture to a woodland edge or specimen planting without
the low, cascading framework of most laceleaf Japanese maples. Its green summer
canopy develops warm autumn color before revealing the branching structure.

Give the roots space away from thirsty competitors, and choose a sheltered
position rather than a hot, exposed corner. Let the natural canopy develop
instead of shearing it into a compact shape.
