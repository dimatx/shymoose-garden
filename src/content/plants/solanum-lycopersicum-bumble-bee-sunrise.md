'Bumble Bee Sunrise' is a standout in the Artisan cherry tomato collection,
producing small, sunny fruit splashed in yellow and red. It is one of the
sweetest of the bumble bee series, balancing that sweetness with a bright,
tangy finish.

## A vigorous producer

These indeterminate plants are high in vigor and keep producing fruit all season,
making them a generous addition to any sunny garden. The variety is grown to USDA
Organic standards.

## Care tips

Give the plants full sun and sturdy support — as an indeterminate, the vines keep
growing and fruiting until frost. Pick the fruit when fully colored for the
sweetest flavor.
