*Helleborus* 'Spanish Flare' is a Lenten rose hybrid grown for its striking,
nodding flowers — pale yellow petals marked with bold maroon flares at the
base — that open in late winter, often before almost anything else is
blooming. Paired with leathery, deep green evergreen foliage, it provides
color and structure through the coldest months of the year.

## An early-blooming shade staple

Lenten roses are prized by shade gardeners for flowering so early, frequently
pushing through late snow or frost to bring color to the garden when little
else is stirring. 'Spanish Flare' keeps its handsome foliage through winter,
so the plant offers year-round interest even between bloom cycles.

## Care tips

Site in partial to full shade in rich, well-drained soil; established plants
tolerate short dry spells but appreciate consistent moisture while in bloom.
In late winter, cut away any tattered old leaves before the new flower stalks
emerge to keep the display looking its best. As with all hellebores, all parts
of the plant are toxic if ingested, and deer and rabbits generally leave it
alone.
