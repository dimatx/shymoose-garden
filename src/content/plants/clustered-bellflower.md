'Superba' is an upright clustered bellflower with rich violet-purple
flowers on stems reaching 2–3 feet. It suits cottage gardens and mixed
borders where it has room to spread.

## Strikingly clustered blooms

Bell-shaped flowers gather in tight clusters at the stem tips, with
smaller clusters below. Bees visit the blooms from late spring into
early summer.

## Care tips

Provide moist, well-drained soil in sun or part shade, with afternoon
shade in hot climates. Deadhead to encourage more bloom. Rhizomes and
self-seeding can spread aggressively; divide clumps and remove unwanted
growth to keep the planting contained.
