'Blue Prince' is a male Meserve (blue) holly, a dense, bushy evergreen shrub
with purplish stems and dark blue-green leaves with spiny-toothed margins.
Missouri Botanical Garden lists it at 8–12 ft tall and wide, hardy in zones
4–7.

## Through the seasons

The glossy evergreen foliage holds its color through winter. Tiny
greenish-white flowers appear in April–May; they are not showy, but they
produce abundant pollen over a long period, making 'Blue Prince' an excellent
pollinator for female blue hollies such as 'Blue Princess'. Being male, it
produces no berries itself.

## Care tips

Grow in average, slightly acidic, moist, well-drained soil in full sun to
part shade, in a spot protected from cold winter winds. Plant one male for
every three to five females if you want berries. Potential problems include
holly leaf miner, spider mites, scale and leaf spot. Sources: Missouri
Botanical Garden Plant Finder (cultivar and species culture), ASPCA
(toxicity).
