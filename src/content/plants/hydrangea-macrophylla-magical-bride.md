Bigleaf hydrangea 'Magical Bride' is a compact, rounded shrub with sturdy
stems for small borders, patio containers, and bright woodland edges.

## White blooms that mellow to mint green

White mopheads gradually turn mint green as they mature and make good cut
flowers. Blooming on both old and new growth extends the display from
summer into early fall.

## Care tips

Provide rich, moist, well-drained soil and protection from hot afternoon
sun. Prune sparingly, removing deadwood in spring or shaping lightly
after bloom to preserve flower buds.
