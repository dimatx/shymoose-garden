*Rodgersia pinnata* 'Superba' is a clump-forming perennial grown mainly for its architectural foliage. In spring the new leaves unfurl bronze, then expand into large, pleated, palm-like leaves that mature to rich green with a bronze tinge. In early to mid-summer, red stems carry feathery plumes of bright pink flowers that resemble a giant astilbe.

## Bold texture for shade

The big leaves give a tropical look and a strong contrast to the fine foliage of ferns, astilbes and many other shade plants. The seed heads hold up well after flowering. At the Chicago Botanic Garden, the drama is described as being in the foliage; the blooms are not profuse or reliable in every climate. It is also left alone by slugs, rabbits and deer.

## Care tips

Moisture is the key: give it deep, rich soil, dappled sun and regular water, and let it get established. It does well near ponds, streams and rain gardens. Mulch generously, avoid spots with competing tree roots and dry wind, and divide in spring if a clump gets too large.
