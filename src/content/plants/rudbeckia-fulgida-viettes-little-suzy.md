*Rudbeckia fulgida* 'Viette's Little Suzy' packs all the charm of a classic
black-eyed Susan into a tidy, compact plant — golden-yellow daisies with dark
brown centers on stems that rarely top 15 inches. It's an ideal choice for
edging a border or filling a small sunny bed without the sprawl of larger
Rudbeckia cultivars.

## Big color, small footprint

Despite its diminutive size, 'Little Suzy' blooms just as generously as its
taller relatives, carrying golden flowers from midsummer well into fall. Its
compact habit makes it useful in spots where full-size black-eyed Susans
would overwhelm the space.

## Care tips

Grow in full sun to light shade in average, well-drained soil. It handles
short dry spells once established, though regular water keeps the display
going strongest. Deadhead through the season to prolong flowering, cut back
after frost, and divide clumps every few years as they thicken.
