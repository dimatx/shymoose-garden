*Astilbe* × arendsii 'Amethyst' brings dense, upright plumes of lilac-purple
flowers over deep green, finely cut foliage — a dependable shade perennial
as long as its one real requirement, steady moisture, is met.

## An original Arends hybrid

'Amethyst' belongs to the Astilbe × arendsii group, bred in the early 1900s
by German nurseryman Georg Arends by crossing several Asian astilbe
species. That breeding program is responsible for the vast majority of
astilbe cultivars grown today, and more than a century later, 'Amethyst'
remains a garden staple.

## Care tips

Site in part to full shade with rich, humus-enriched soil that stays evenly
moist — astilbes decline quickly in dry soil, showing scorched, browning
foliage. Mulching helps conserve moisture through summer heat. Leave the
spent flower plumes standing for winter texture, or cut them back for
tidiness, and divide congested clumps every three to four years in early
spring to keep the planting vigorous.
