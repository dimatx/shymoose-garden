*Heuchera* Dolce® 'Cherry Truffles' is a coral bells grown above all for its
foliage: a low, tidy mound of large, heavily ruffled leaves that emerge a vivid
cherry red in spring and deepen to a rich mahogany red as the season goes on.
Because older leaves darken while new ones keep coming in bright, the plant
reads as a shifting blend of red tones from spring through fall.

Unusually adaptable, it holds up in everything from full sun to full shade,
keeping its best color where it gets a little light. In midsummer it sends up
airy sprays of small light-pink flowers on slender stems that hover over the
foliage and pull in bees, butterflies, and hummingbirds. Give it humus-rich,
well-drained soil, tidy the foliage in early spring, and it will return
reliably year after year — in a border, a container, or massed along a shaded
path.
