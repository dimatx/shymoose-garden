*Daphne* × *burkwoodii* 'Carol Mackie' is a compact, rounded, semi-evergreen shrub with small leaves edged in cream-gold. In mid-spring it is covered with clusters of very fragrant pale pink flowers that can be smelled from several feet away, and it may bloom lightly again in fall.

## Placement matters

Daphnes can be finicky. They want moist but very well-drained soil, light shade, and shelter from cold winds. They resent being moved, so choose the spot carefully, ideally beside a path, entry or seating area where the scent can be enjoyed. At 3–4 feet it suits foundation plantings, borders, slopes and small spaces.

## Care tips

Mulch to keep roots cool, water only when needed, and skip heavy fertilizer. Prune only to remove dead branches. The plant can be short-lived and sometimes fails without obvious cause. The red berries and other parts are poisonous, so keep pets and children away.
