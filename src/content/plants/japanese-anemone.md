*Anemone* 'Whirlwind' brings a late-season lift to the shade garden: airy,
semi-double white flowers with bright yellow centers that appear to float on
tall, wiry stems well above the foliage, from late summer right through fall.

## A long-lived heritage cultivar

'Whirlwind' isn't a new introduction — it dates back to the 1880s, bred by
the French nurseryman Victor Lemoine from crosses of *Anemone hupehensis*
and *Anemone vitifolia*. Its dependable, weather-resistant blooms have kept
it in cultivation ever since, and it remains one of the most popular white
Japanese anemones sold today.

## Care tips

Site in part shade with rich, evenly moist soil — it tolerates more sun if
the soil never dries out. Foliage emerges late in spring, which makes it a
good companion for spring bulbs as their leaves die back. Once established,
avoid moving or dividing the clump; Japanese anemones resent root
disturbance but will slowly naturalize into a larger colony if left alone.
Cut back the dead foliage after frost each fall.
