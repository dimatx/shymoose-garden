Ostrich fern 'Jumbo' is a bold, deciduous fern for cool, moist shade. Its
upright green fronds unfurl from tight fiddleheads in spring, then spread
outward to form a broad, vase-shaped clump.

## Lush fronds for moist shade

The cultivar grows larger than the species, with green fronds reaching
several feet tall and a clump that can widen over time. Separate, upright
fertile fronds carry spores and often remain visible after the green fronds
have died back. The rhizomes spread readily, so give the plant room or
contain it where a colony is not wanted.

## Care tips

Choose a shaded site with rich soil that stays consistently moist. Ostrich
fern can grow at the edge of a pond or in other wet places, but it is a poor
choice for dry shade. Remove old fronds before new fiddleheads emerge in
spring.
