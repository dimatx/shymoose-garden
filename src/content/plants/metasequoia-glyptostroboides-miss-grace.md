'Miss Grace' is a weeping selection of dawn redwood, a deciduous conifer
from China that was thought extinct until living trees turned up in the
1940s. Left unstaked, it forms a sprawling mound; trained on a stake, it
becomes a small weeping tree, roughly 10 ft tall and 3 ft wide after 10 years.

## Through the seasons

Soft, feathery, gray-green needles cover the cascading branches through
summer, then turn orange in autumn before the whole tree drops them. In
winter the bare, weeping framework takes center stage.
The cultivar was found as a witch's broom in New York and introduced by
Buchholz & Buchholz Nursery in Oregon.

## Care tips

Give it full sun and moist, well-drained, slightly acidic soil, and keep it
watered in dry weather. It transplants easily. Stake the leader if you want
a small weeping tree rather than a sprawling mound.
