*Symphyotrichum* 'Wood's Purple' is a compact New York aster that erupts
into a mound of small violet-purple daisies just as most of the garden is
winding down, providing one of the last big pollinator meals of the season.

## Bred to stay clean and compact

Taller, older aster varieties are notorious for powdery mildew by late
summer. 'Wood's Purple' belongs to the "Wood's" series, bred specifically to
resist that disease while staying dense and compact — no staking or
pinching required to keep it tidy.

## Care tips

Plant in full sun in average, well-drained soil with good air circulation
to further discourage mildew. Water regularly, especially during dry
spells, though established plants tolerate some drought. Cut the plant back
to the ground after it finishes flowering, and divide congested clumps every
two to three years in spring to keep it vigorous and floriferous.
