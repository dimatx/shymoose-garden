*Ligularia dentata* 'Osiris Fantaisie' is a patented cultivar (U.S. Plant
Patent PP19,302) of leopard plant, bred by Serge Farard of Les Jardins Osiris
in Quebec, Canada. Like the species — a clump-forming perennial in the daisy
family native to moist mountain woodlands of China and Japan — it is grown as
much for its dramatic foliage as its flowers, but in a more compact package
than the straight species.

## Bold foliage, bright flowers

Thick, leathery, deeply veined leaves with incised, undulating margins are
dark green above and burgundy underneath, forming a basal mound up to 24 in
tall and wide. In mid- to late summer, reddish-purple stems rise above the
foliage carrying loose clusters of bright orange-yellow daisy flowers 2–3 in
across, drawing bees and butterflies.

## Care tips

This is a plant for the moist, shady corner. Give it rich soil that never
dries out — a pond or stream edge is ideal — and shelter from hot afternoon
sun, which makes the leaves wilt dramatically (they recover once watered or
as the sun moves off). Deadhead spent stalks and cut the foliage back after
frost.
