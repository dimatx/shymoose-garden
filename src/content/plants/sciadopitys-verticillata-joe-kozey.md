'Joe Kozey' is a narrow, upright selection of Japanese umbrella pine,
introduced by Sidney Waxman at the University of Connecticut. Oregon State's
Landscape Plants notes that at 20 ft tall it is only about 6 ft wide, with
sturdy branches held tight to the trunk that can withstand heavy snow loads.

## Through the seasons

It is evergreen: glossy green needles in whorls give a year-round, spire-like
silhouette that grows slowly into a large tree. Reddish-brown bark that
exfoliates in strips adds interest on older trees.

## Care tips

Plant in rich, moist, well-drained, slightly acidic soil in sun or part
shade, sheltered from harsh winter wind. NC State Extension notes the species
is not drought or pollution tolerant and does best in cool summers, with
afternoon shade in very hot ones. Sources: Oregon State Landscape Plants and
Iseli Nursery (cultivar), NC State Extension Plant Toolbox (species culture).

## Pet safety

**Unknown for dogs and cats.** [Van den Berk Nurseries](https://www.vdberk.com/trees/sciadopitys-verticillata/)
rates the species usually non-toxic to pets, but no dog/cat-specific
veterinary assessment was verified.
