*Pinus strobus* 'Angel Falls' is a weeping cultivar of eastern white pine, a
North American conifer in the pine family (Pinaceae). Where the species grows
into a towering forest tree 50–80 ft tall, 'Angel Falls' is a garden-scaled
selection prized for its cascading habit: long, soft, blue-green needles in
bundles of five drape from pendulous branches like a green waterfall.

## A living sculpture

Young plants are typically staked to a desired height, after which the
weeping branches are allowed to spill downward, creating a distinctive
fountain or curtain shape. Left unstaked, it sprawls as a low, mounding
groundcover instead — either way it makes a striking accent or specimen
planting.

## Care tips

Give it full sun and well-drained, acidic soil for the healthiest color and
form; it is intolerant of urban pollution and alkaline soil, both of which can
cause the needles to yellow. Otherwise it is a low-fuss conifer: water during
establishment and dry spells, and prune only the current season's candle
growth if you want to control size.
