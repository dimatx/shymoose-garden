*Geum chiloense* 'Mrs. Bradshaw' — commonly called Avens — is a rhizomatous herbaceous perennial in the rose family (Rosaceae), native to Chile. It forms a neat, mounding rosette of large, hairy, pinnately divided dark green leaves that provides attractive ground cover even when not in flower.

## Prolific summer blooms

From late spring through summer, branching wiry stems rise 12–18 inches above the foliage and carry semi-double to double scarlet-orange blooms roughly an inch across. 'Mrs. Bradshaw' is one of the most popular cultivars for its vivid color and reliable repeat-flowering, especially when deadheaded. After the petals drop, the feathery plumed seed heads extend ornamental interest through fall.

## Care tips

Avens performs best in cool climates with good drainage. It tolerates some drought but cannot sit in wet soil over winter — root rot is the most common problem. In hot, humid regions, give it afternoon shade and divide clumps every year or two to prevent die-out and keep the display lush. A low-maintenance perennial once established, it is ignored by deer and draws bees and butterflies throughout its long blooming season.
