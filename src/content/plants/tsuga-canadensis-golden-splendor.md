'Golden Splendor' resembles the native Canadian (eastern) hemlock in all but
color: its foliage is golden. It was introduced by John Mitsch of Aurora,
Oregon, and grows more than 15 in. per year to about
25 ft tall and 15 ft wide, hardy to zone 4.

## Through the seasons

Bright golden-yellow, softly drooping foliage glows year-round. It
tolerates full sun and produces brighter color with more exposure.
Planted in a row, it can be sheared into
a golden hedge.

## Care tips

Plant in moist but well-drained, somewhat acidic soil in sun to part shade.
It grows best where summers are cool and does not tolerate drought.
Check the undersides of branches for the white, cottony egg masses of
hemlock woolly adelgid, a major pest.
