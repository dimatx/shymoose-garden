'Junior Walker' is a compact, sterile catmint for sunny borders, path
edges, and containers. Its low, spreading mound stays smaller than
'Walker's Low' and does not spread by seed.

## Fragrant foliage, long-season color

Aromatic gray-green leaves soften the edge of a bed, while lavender-blue
flowers attract bees from late spring into summer. Some cats investigate
the foliage, but this is not the catnip species.

## Care tips

Provide full sun and freely draining soil; established plants tolerate
drought. Shear back by one-third to one-half after the first bloom to
encourage fresh growth and repeat flowers. Avoid wet winter soil,
which can cause crown rot.
