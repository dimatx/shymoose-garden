Hellebore 'Snow Love' is an evergreen, winter-blooming hybrid selected for
its compact habit and pale flowers. Rounded, leathery leaves form a low mound,
while the blooms open outward above the foliage.

## Flowers for the end of winter

The flowers begin a soft yellow-green and lighten toward ivory as they age.
They can open in late winter, often before the rest of the garden has
reawakened, and continue into spring.

## Care tips

Plant Snow Love in humus-rich, well-drained soil in part shade. Keep it
moderately moist while it establishes, then water during prolonged dry
spells. Before the buds open, remove old or damaged leaves to show the
flowers; cut back spent flower stems after blooming.

> **Heads up:** all parts of hellebores are toxic if eaten, especially the
> roots. Keep the plant away from curious pets and children.
